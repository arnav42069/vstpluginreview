## Article-level briefs

### 01 — How to Make a Psytrance Kick That Cuts Through the Mix
- Slug: `how-to-make-a-psytrance-kick`
- Audience: intermediate psytrance producer struggling with muddy or weak kick.
- Thesis: get pitch trajectory, amp contour, harmonics and kick/bass relationship right before applying excessive processing.
- Sections: target reference; source waveform; pitch envelope; gain envelope; saturation; phase/bass fit; troubleshooting; checklist.
- Visual asset: hand-drawn pitch-vs-time envelope, original DAW screenshots (not generated plugin UI).
- Audio: raw sine pitch-sweep versus processed kick, RMS or loudness level matched for fair evaluation.
- Internal links: 02, 04.

### 02 — Kick and Bass Phase Alignment: A Practical Guide
- Slug: `kick-bass-phase-alignment`
- Audience: producers whose low end disappears or booms unexpectedly.
- Thesis: polarity is not phase alignment; compare timing, envelope and frequency overlap in context rather than applying fixed offsets blindly.
- Sections: phase and polarity; low-frequency overlap; monitoring; test workflow; dangers of solo-only decisions.
- Visual: original diagram showing shifted periodic waveforms.
- Audio: kick plus bass variants at equal levels, annotated with sample offset.
- Internal links: 01, 05.

### 03 — Best Free VST Plugins for EDM Producers: A Useful Starter Toolkit
- Slug: `best-free-vst-plugins-edm`
- Audience: beginner or budget-conscious producer.
- Thesis: select a small complete toolkit; avoid bloated lists, avoid claiming all plugins were personally tested.
- Source-check official products before publication: Vital (https://vital.audio/), Surge XT (https://surge-synthesizer.github.io/), TDR Nova (https://www.tokyodawn.net/tdr-nova/), Valhalla Supermassive (https://valhalladsp.com/shop/reverb/valhalla-supermassive/), and MeldaProduction MFreeFXBundle (https://www.meldaproduction.com/MFreeFXBundle).
- Visual: original category comparison matrix.
- CTA: no manufactured download; link to each official product.
- Internal links: 01, 05.

### 04 — Kick Synthesizer vs Samples: Which Should You Use?
- Slug: `kick-synthesizer-vs-samples`
- Audience: producers considering investing in a dedicated kick plugin.
- Thesis: a synth improves repeatability/control, while good samples are faster and may be entirely sufficient; both have costs.
- Product tie-in: disclose Azazel Audio ownership before Suckerpunch mention. Do not assert Suckerpunch capabilities outside verified docs.
- Add independent A/B sound demo before upgrading to product review content.
- Internal links: 01, 02.

### 05 — How to Compress Drums Without Losing Punch
- Slug: `compress-drums-without-losing-punch`
- Audience: intermediate EDM producer.
- Thesis: attack, release, threshold and gain matching matter more than aggressive gain reduction.
- Visual: transient shape before/after compression.
- Audio: true gain-matched processed/bypassed loops.
- CTA: transparent mention of VisualComp as an Azazel Audio product; no unsupported feature claims.
- Internal links: 01, 02.
