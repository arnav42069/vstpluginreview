## Remaining review limits

No tool was installed or audibly tested for this guide. All buying recommendations are inferences from the documented controls and stated user task. Only dated, clearly displayed prices are quoted; regional checkout, tax and promotion variations are stated. Some version/minimum-OS lists are inherently stale until developer updates; readers must verify their actual host support. Future article updates should rerun official-source checks, distinguish product-name changes (notably Mu/VM-Comp), and record substantive modified dates rather than automatic freshness dates.
